# Reasoning before the 2041 election (after 2038)

## What 2038 showed

- **MMP**: overhangs still 4–23 seats (median 12; Houses up to 144). In world 5
  the left won 54% of the vote but the right won the majority (70 of 139),
  carried by National's 11-seat overhang.
- **MMP-T / MMP-BV**: overhang fell to a median of 2–4. Where Green and
  Opportunity voters consolidated behind Labour in marginals, National's
  plurality overhang shrank (world 5: 11 → 1; world 9: 10 → 0). It persisted
  where National is very strong and the left is fragmented (worlds 2 and 3:
  9–15 seats, still absorbed). In left landslides Labour itself now overhangs
  (worlds 4, 7, 9), and the mirror case appears for ACT and NZ First voters.
  TPM's party vote is now 4–11%, so its overhang is mostly gone.

## How voters respond

Incentives are unchanged from 2038, and the voters who saw consolidation work
keep doing it: the same rules are applied again from the new starting point
(consolidation in marginals where the other side's leader overhangs, up to a
split rate of 0.35; left personal splitting keeps easing; TPM closes its gap
where it still overhangs). Under MMP the arms race continues as before.
