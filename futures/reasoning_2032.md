# Reasoning before the 2032 election (after 2029)

> **Note (official-count rerun, 27 November 2026):** these futures now start from the official 2026 count; they first started from the preliminary count. The reasoning rules are unchanged, and follow roles (left leader, right partners, …) rather than party names, so they keep working if, say, the Greens overtake Labour. The figures below come from this rerun and match the digests in this folder.

## What 2029 showed

- **Under MMP** every world had an overhang, of 5–18 seats, and a House of
  126–139 seats. The two engines were the same as in 2026: National winning
  far more electorates than its party vote (ACT and NZ First voters ticking
  National candidates), and Te Pāti Māori winning 6–7 Māori seats on
  0.3–1.1% of the party vote (Labour and Green voters ticking TPM
  candidates; TPM's own supporters party-voting Green or Labour).
- **Under MMP-T and MMP-BV** (the first election under each) voters had
  barely changed behaviour (awareness 0.2), so the same overhangs had to be
  transferred: 38,000–288,000 party votes moved. Where National overhung,
  ACT lost 2–5 seats and NZ First up to 4; Labour lost 2–5 and the Greens
  1–4 (some through their voters' personal votes for National MPs, most
  through the TPM transfer). TPM's overhang could not be covered even with
  every eligible ballot, so it was absorbed in all 20 reform worlds: the left
  paid for TPM's seats in list seats. National's overhang was absorbed too in
  5 of the 20.

## How each group of voters responds

**MMP worlds.** Splitting works and nothing punishes it.
- *ACT and NZ First voters* keep ticking National candidates; a little more
  each time National overhangs (general rule, +0.03).
- *Green voters*: where the right won more seats than votes and the majority,
  left strategists run "tick Labour in marginals" campaigns; Green voters'
  split rate rises by 40%. Not fully rational — many Greens still tick Green.
- *National voters*: where the left won the majority on an overhang, National
  copies the cup-of-tea approach more openly: about a point of National's
  vote goes to ACT or NZ First's list while those voters keep ticking the
  National candidate.
- *Māori seats*: the TPM strategy paid again; it continues.

**MMP-T and MMP-BV worlds.** The 2029 transfers were large and public.
- *ACT and NZ First* lost seats because their voters ticked National
  candidates. Both parties run "two ticks" campaigns. Their voters are
  engaged, so the response is fast: split rates fall 45% (30% where they lost
  fewer than 3 seats), on top of the general learning rule.
- *Green and Opportunity voters* respond more slowly (25% cut): their splits
  are more often personal (a liked local MP) than tactical.
- *Te Pāti Māori*: its electorates no longer bring the left extra seats and
  the left pays for them. TPM switches to asking for the party vote. That's
  party-led, so faster than ordinary voter learning: it recovers about half
  the gap to its 2023 party vote (3.1%), taken from Green and Labour.
- *Labour and Green voters in Māori seats* partly return to two ticks
  (s_M 0.40 → 0.32).
- *MMP-BV only*: the backup vote is starting to be understood (awareness 0.5
  after a visible first election): sub-threshold parties' voters fear wasting
  their vote less, and pick a qualifying candidate for their electorate vote.

## Addendum: the -X systems (MMP-T-X, MMP-BV-X and their 141 versions)

Under -X, overhang the transfer can't cover is kept and the House grows, as
under MMP. That changes two incentives:
- *Te Pāti Māori*: its split-ballot transfer takes Labour and Green party votes
  (a cost within the left), but whatever can't be covered still adds seats for
  the left. The strategy still pays, so TPM does **not** switch to asking for
  the party vote. The Greens, whose voters' party votes are being transferred
  to TPM, push for two ticks in Māori seats: s_M eases slowly (−0.02 a round).
- *ACT and NZ First*: their voters' split ballots for National winners are
  still transferred first, so the same two-tick response applies as under
  MMP-T / MMP-BV.
